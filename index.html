Since 1948

Seventy-eight years of student journalism

From a mimeographed newsletter to a multi-platform publication, The Work has been the university's independent student voice through every era of TSU history.

1948

Founded

The Work is established as the official student publication of Tarlac State University.

1991

Republic Act 7079

The Campus Journalism Act strengthens student publications nationwide.

2016

Digital expansion

The Work broadens its reach through digital platforms.

2019

First RHEPC overall championship

First overall championship at the 18th RHEPC.

2020

Back-to-back champions

Second consecutive overall championship at the 19th RHEPC.

2025

Best Performing Publication

Named Best Performing Publication at the 23rd RHEPC.

Today

78th generation

The current editorial board continues the work of every generation before it.

What we publish

Regular issues and literary folios

The Work circulates its regular issue at least four times in an academic year and publishes its literary folio, Obra, once a year.
